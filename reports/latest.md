## Methodology

`value_score` is a **relative** index within one report (best plan = 100):

1. `USD/mo` — listed price ÷ FX rate, ÷ 12 when billed annually.
2. `capacity` — monthly tokens (weight per `scoring.yaml`) and premium requests, log-scaled across plans. Quotas published per 5h/day/week use fixed monthly factors (5h→×144) and are flagged as heavy-use estimates, not guarantees.
3. `feature` — weighted checklist (agent, cli, api, ide, model-choice, mcp, team).
4. `value` — `0.7·capacity + 0.3·feature`, divided by effective price, normalized to 100. Free tiers use a small floor price so they cannot dominate by dividing by zero.

Quotas shown as estimates come from an LLM reading the page or from unit conversion; treat them as directional. The JSON export keeps every raw field for audit.
